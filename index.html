Privacy Policy

App: Stash - Personal Finance Tracker | Effective Date: August 8, 2026

🔒 Zero Data Transmission Policy: Stash operates entirely on your device. Your financial messages, transactional alerts, and budget records are processed strictly in local memory and stored on your local SQLite database. We do not own external servers, and your data never leaves your device.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Stash ("we", "our", or "us"). Stash is a privacy-first personal finance tracking application built to help users seamlessly monitor expenses, set budgets, and gain financial control.

We respect your privacy unconditionally. All message parsing, notification reading, transaction recording, and analytics computations are performed 100% locally on your smartphone. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your personal or financial data on external servers.

2. Information We Access and Process

To provide automated expense logging, Stash requests specific device permissions. Here is how data is accessed and used:

A. SMS Messages (READ_SMS & RECEIVE_SMS)

  • What is Accessed: Incoming transactional text messages from financial entities (banks, credit cards, payment gateways).
  • How it is Processed: In real-time via Android's BroadcastReceiver when an SMS arrives or during manual inbox sync.
  • Purpose: To automatically detect transaction amounts, merchant names, debit/credit flags, and timestamps. Personal non-financial SMS messages (OTP codes, personal chats) are ignored.
  • Storage: Parsed transaction summaries are saved strictly in your device's local database. Raw SMS content is never transmitted off your phone.

B. Notification Listener Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

  • What is Accessed: Payment alerts posted by financial & payment applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, mobile banking apps).
  • Purpose: To auto-log instant payments as expenses or income. Non-financial notifications are filtered out immediately.
  • Storage: Extracted transaction records are saved exclusively to local device storage.

C. User-Entered Data

  • What is Collected: Manual transactions, budget targets, custom category tags, and account settings created by you.
  • Storage: Stored strictly on your local SQLite database.

D. Local File Storage (Export / Import)

  • What is Accessed: Files explicitly selected by you for exporting CSV/Excel financial summaries or restoring backup databases.

3. Why We Process This Data

Data processing is restricted exclusively to core app features requested by the user:

  • Automated expense and income transaction logging from bank SMS and UPI payment alerts.
  • On-device budgeting, spending analytics, and category breakdowns.
  • Local notifications and streak reminders.

4. Zero Third-Party Sharing & No Analytics

We strictly guarantee:

  • Zero Data Selling or Sharing: Your information is never sold, shared, or traded.
  • No External Cloud Servers: We maintain no cloud backend, external databases, or remote web services.
  • No Analytics SDKs: Stash contains no third-party tracking or telemetry tools (e.g., no Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel).
  • No Ad Networks: Stash is 100% ad-free and contains no advertising networks (e.g., no AdMob).

5. Data Retention & User Control

Because all data remains on your device, you possess total control:

  • Editing & Deleting: You can edit or remove any transaction or category within Stash at any time.
  • Wiping App Storage: Clear all data anytime via Android System Settings (Settings > Apps > Stash > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling Stash permanently and permanently destroys all local database files. Cloud recovery is impossible because no backups are stored remotely.

6. Permission Management

SMS and Notification permissions are completely voluntary. You can grant or revoke permission anytime via Android Settings:

  • SMS Permissions: Settings > Apps > Stash > Permissions > SMS
  • Notification Access: Settings > Apps > Special app access > Notification access > Stash

If permissions are denied or revoked, Stash remains fully usable as a manual finance tracker.

7. Compliance with Google Play Policies

Stash complies with Google Play's SMS and Call Log Permissions Policy and Financial Features Policy. SMS and Notification permissions are requested exclusively to deliver core personal finance tracking features. All data remains local, non-transferable, and secure.
