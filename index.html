Privacy Policy

Stash - Personal Finance Tracker · Effective October 29, 2026 · Updated October 29, 2026

About Stash

Stash is an offline-first personal finance tracker for recording transactions, planning budgets, reviewing spending, and managing savings goals. Stash does not require an account and does not provide banking, payment, lending, investment, or credit services.

Information stored on your device

Stash stores information you enter or create in its local app database, including transactions, balances, budget settings, categories, savings goals, and app preferences. You can import or export transaction files using the system file picker.

Optional payment-app notification logging

If you enable auto-logging and grant Android Notification Access, Android provides Stash access to device notifications. Stash processes notifications only from its supported payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED, Amazon Pay, BHIM, and BHIM SBI Pay) to identify transaction alerts. Android's notification listener can receive notification events from the device, but Stash does not parse or save notifications from other apps. Stash does not access SMS messages or the SMS inbox.

When Stash is not open, up to 50 supported payment-app notification events may be temporarily buffered in private app storage for processing when Stash next opens. The buffer is cleared after processing or when auto-logging is turned off. Extracted transaction details are stored in Stash's local database. Notification Access is optional; manual entry, budgeting, analytics, savings goals, and statement import remain available without it.

You can revoke Notification Access in Android Settings under Special app access > Notification access.

Use and sharing

Information is used to provide Stash's on-device finance tracking features. Stash has no account system or cloud sync and does not send transaction records or notification contents to a Stash server. Stash does not include advertising or analytics features and does not sell or share information for advertising or marketing.

File import and export use the system file picker. You choose which file Stash reads and where an exported file is saved.

Retention and deletion

Information remains in local app storage until you delete individual records in Stash, clear Stash's storage in Android Settings (Settings > Apps > Stash > Storage > Clear data), or uninstall Stash. Temporarily buffered payment notification events are removed after processing or when auto-logging is turned off. Uninstalling removes local app data; Stash has no cloud copy to restore.

Security

Stash stores its database and temporary notification buffer in private app storage managed by Android. No local storage method can guarantee absolute security; protect your device with its screen lock and keep Android up to date.

Children

Stash is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Stash does not require users to submit personal information to an account.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data handling change. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on Stash's Google Play listing.
